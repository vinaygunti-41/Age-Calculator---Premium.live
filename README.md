#  Age Calculator

A modern, glassmorphism-styled age calculator that tells you exactly how long you've been alive — down to the second — and counts down to your next birthday. Built with nothing but HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## ✨ Features

- **Full age breakdown** — years, months, and days, plus running totals in months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds
- **Live running counter** — the total-seconds stat ticks upward in real time instead of freezing at calculation
- **Next birthday countdown** — days and months remaining, the exact date, and the day of the week
- **Add to calendar** — saves your next birthday as a repeating `.ics` calendar event, or opens a pre-filled Google Calendar link as a fallback
- **Birth details** — day of the week you were born, birth year, current year, and zodiac sign
- **Age in different units** — a dedicated grid converting your age into years, months, weeks, days, hours, and minutes
- **Day / night theme toggle** — a green-and-white light theme and a dark green-on-charcoal night theme, remembered between visits
- **Live clock** — the header shows today's date alongside a ticking real-time clock
- **Input validation** — catches empty fields, future dates, and invalid dates with clear inline messages
- **Fully responsive** — rearranges gracefully across desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Zero dependencies** — no frameworks, no build step, just three files
